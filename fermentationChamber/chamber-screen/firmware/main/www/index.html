Humidity

target RH %
Runs the compressor to condense moisture while the heater cancels the cooling, so RH falls without the temperature moving. Only sensible on a chamber whose evaporator drains. Aborts if the temperature strays beyond its limit, and rests after a duty cap.

Inputs & probe

calibrate internal probe:
Put the probe and the Pill in the same place and let them settle. One point gives a constant offset; a second point at a substantially different temperature (5 °C or more apart) fits a slope. The Pill is factory-calibrated, so it makes a good reference.

Firmware update

Writes the inactive OTA slot. If the new build fails to come up and mark itself valid, the bootloader rolls back automatically.
